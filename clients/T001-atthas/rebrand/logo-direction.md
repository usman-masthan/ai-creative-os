# ATTHA'S Logo Direction — Rebrand V1

**Status:** Concept direction — visual logo not yet approved

## Recommended direction: The Flavour Mark

Preserve the recognisable idea of the legacy **A + fork**, but simplify it into a modern monogram that can work at app-icon, signboard and packaging scale.

### Construction principles

- Custom `A` monogram rather than a stock script letter.
- Fork reference should be subtle and structural, not a clip-art utensil.
- Master wordmark reads `ATTHA'S` clearly at small sizes.
- `RESTAURANT` and `BURGER` become clean descriptors beneath or beside the master wordmark.
- Avoid burger illustrations inside the logo; let food photography carry appetite.
- Must work in one colour before colour variants are approved.

## Logo family

1. Master: `ATTHA'S`
2. Restaurant lock-up: `ATTHA'S / RESTAURANT`
3. Burger lock-up: `ATTHA'S / BURGER`
4. Icon: simplified A/flavour monogram

## Why this direction

It keeps recognition from the old identity while moving away from the visually busy fast-food-menu aesthetic. It also gives the Restaurant and Burger businesses one scalable brand system instead of separate unrelated logos.
