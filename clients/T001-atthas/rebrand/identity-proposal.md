# ATTHA'S Rebrand Identity Proposal V1

## Goal

Move ATTHA'S from a loud red/yellow takeaway look into a **premium, heartfelt, flavour-led brand** that still has enough energy for burgers and social media.

## Colour system

- `#8F1D14` — Deep Red: heritage, appetite, confidence
- `#F3B61F` — Flame Gold: flavour, warmth, highlight
- `#FFF4E3` — Warm Cream: premium warmth and breathing room
- `#1D1917` — Charcoal: sophisticated contrast
- `#3E6B4F` — Herb Green: Restaurant-only freshness accent
- `#D9412C` — Chilli Red: Burger-only high-energy accent

## Typography

- **Fraunces SemiBold** — premium master/editorial display
- **Manrope** — body, prices, menus, UI and digital readability
- **Barlow Condensed Bold** — Burger-only campaign punch

## Photography

- natural food texture
- directional warm light
- rich but believable saturation
- real ingredients
- close appetite detail
- human/table context for Restaurant
- strong hero isolation for Burger

## Layout principle

Less clutter, more hierarchy: **one hero, one message, one CTA**.
