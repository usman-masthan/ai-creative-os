# Visual and Photography System

## Visual idea

**Flavour in full view.** Food is the hero; brand devices frame and amplify it rather than covering it.

## Photography

### Shared requirements

- Real menu item or clearly labelled concept image.
- Fresh texture, accurate ingredients and believable portions.
- Warm directional light with clean highlights and natural shadows.
- Controlled saturation: appetising reds and golds without neon food.
- Product edges remain crisp; sauces retain texture.
- Leave intentional negative space for copy.

### Burger

- Eye-level or low three-quarter hero angle.
- Strong stack silhouette and visible layers.
- Optional motion: sauce drizzle, crumbs, steam or ingredient lift—never all at once.
- Deep-red or warm-neutral background with high contrast.
- Bold crops are allowed when the whole product is shown elsewhere in the campaign.

### Restaurant

- Table-level, overhead or intimate three-quarter compositions.
- Show serving context, tableware and shared dining when relevant.
- Warm cream, timber, charcoal or deep-red environments.
- More breathing room and softer contrast than Burger.
- Represent cuisines accurately; do not mix culturally unrelated props as decoration.

## Image restrictions

- No fake cheese stretch, impossible ingredient geometry or plastic-looking meat.
- No random utensils embedded in food.
- No AI-generated text, labels, packaging or logos.
- No reference-image product should be presented as the actual ATTHA’S product without verification.
- No competing food-brand packaging.
- Do not hide portion size using extreme perspective.

## Graphic devices

- Brush edge: energetic separator for Burger; use sparingly.
- Thin red rule: information separator for menus and Restaurant layouts.
- Gold burst: new item or limited highlight; one per panel maximum.
- Rounded information card: white/cream field, red 1 px border, 8–12 px radius.
- Dotted leader: menus and price lists only.

## Icons

- Use a consistent 2 px rounded stroke at a 24 px base grid.
- Prefer simple filled accents only for flame, location, delivery and dietary status.
- Default icon colour: deep red on light; white/gold on deep red.
- Dietary and halal symbols must be approved and factually applicable.
- Never mix outline, emoji, clip-art and photoreal icons in one execution.

## Backgrounds

Approved: solid deep red, white, warm cream, charcoal, restrained red-to-ember gradient, softly lit material surface.  
Conditional: subtle grain or brush texture below 10% opacity.  
Avoid: busy patterns, generic flames behind every product, rainbow gradients, heavy lens flares and textured fields that reduce legibility.
