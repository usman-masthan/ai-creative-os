# Brand Architecture

## Model

ATTHA’S uses an endorsed sub-brand system. Restaurant and Burger share the master name, recognition assets and trust, while expressing different dining occasions.

## Shared core

**Promise:** Generous food made with passion for flavour and served with warmth.  
**Values:** Generosity, flavour, care, consistency, accessibility and local relevance.  
**Shared signals:** ATTHA’S name, A/fork symbol, deep red, flame gold, confident food presentation and direct hospitality.

## Personality spectrum

| Dimension | Master | Restaurant | Burger |
|---|---|---|---|
| Energy | Confident | Composed | High-energy |
| Hospitality | Warm | Personal and attentive | Fast and friendly |
| Visual density | Balanced | Spacious | Bold and compact |
| Language | Clear | Descriptive and inviting | Short and punchy |
| Food focus | Flavour and generosity | Authentic variety and shared dining | Craveability and indulgence |
| Primary palette | Red/gold/white | Cream/ember/gold | Deep red/flame gold/white |

## Naming

- Legal/first mention: `ATTHA’S Restaurant — Wellawatte` or `ATTHA’S Burger — Wellampitiya`.
- Marketing mention: `ATTHA’S Restaurant` or `ATTHA’S Burger`.
- Branch name never replaces the brand name.
- Offers must state the relevant sub-brand and branch scope.

## Cross-brand rule

Do not mix Burger food styling, slang or menu treatments into Restaurant communications unless the campaign explicitly features both. A master-brand campaign may feature both, but each product must retain its correct name, branch availability and source-approved photography.
