# Layout, Spacing and CTA System

## Base grid

Use a 4 px base unit. All spacing, corner radii and component dimensions should be multiples of four unless print production requires optical adjustment.

| Token | Value | Typical use |
|---|---:|---|
| `space-1` | 4 px | Micro gaps |
| `space-2` | 8 px | Icon-to-label |
| `space-3` | 12 px | Compact controls |
| `space-4` | 16 px | Card padding minimum |
| `space-6` | 24 px | Component groups |
| `space-8` | 32 px | Section spacing on mobile |
| `space-12` | 48 px | Desktop section spacing |
| `space-16` | 64 px | Major content divisions |
| `space-24` | 96 px | Hero breathing room |

## Layout rules

- Digital: 12-column desktop, 8-column tablet, 4-column mobile.
- Menu: strong top-level grouping, stable price alignment and no more than three nested hierarchy levels.
- Keep outer safe area at least 5% of the shortest canvas edge for social artwork.
- Do not place critical copy or logos within platform crop zones.
- Align related names, descriptions and prices to a shared grid.

## CTA component

### Primary Burger CTA

- Background: flame gold
- Text: charcoal ink
- Shape: 8 px radius or approved brand-specific radius
- Type: Inter Bold or Oswald SemiBold
- Minimum digital height: 48 px
- Hover: toasted gold; focus ring must remain visible

### Primary Restaurant CTA

- Background: deep red
- Text: white
- Optional gold keyline for premium contexts
- Shape: 8 px radius
- Minimum digital height: 48 px

### Secondary CTA

- Transparent or white/cream background
- Deep-red label and 1–2 px deep-red border
- Must not visually compete with the primary action

## Social template zones

1. Brand zone: logo and sub-brand descriptor.
2. Appetite zone: hero product or dining scene.
3. Message zone: one headline and optional supporting line.
4. Action zone: price/offer, CTA, platform and branch scope.
5. Compliance zone: conditions, effective date and required marks.
