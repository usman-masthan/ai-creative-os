# Tone of Voice

## Shared voice

ATTHA’S sounds warm, confident, flavour-led and uncomplicated. It speaks like a generous host, not a corporation.

### Principles

1. Lead with the appetite or human benefit.
2. Use specific sensory language only when it is factually supported.
3. Prefer clear invitations over exaggerated claims.
4. Keep prices, conditions and branch availability easy to find.
5. Never invent ingredients, awards, health benefits or cultural authenticity claims.

## Burger personality

**Bold, playful, energetic, craveable.** Use short sentences, active verbs and rhythmic naming.

Examples:

- “Bring on the crunch.”
- “Double the stack. Double the satisfaction.”
- “Pick your heat.”
- CTA: “Order your burger” / “Build your stack” / “Get it delivered”

Avoid childish humour, aggression, forced American slang and unsupported superlatives such as “Sri Lanka’s best.”

## Restaurant personality

**Warm, genuine, considered, welcoming.** Use richer but restrained descriptions and language suited to families, groups and shared meals.

Examples:

- “Gather around authentic flavours.”
- “A generous table, made for sharing.”
- “Discover today’s selection.”
- CTA: “Reserve your table” / “View the menu” / “Plan your visit”

Avoid overly formal luxury language, vague poetry and claims of authenticity without a defined cuisine or source.

## CTA rules

- Begin with a verb.
- Use two to four words where possible.
- One primary CTA per panel; secondary CTA must be visually quieter.
- Delivery CTAs must name the correct platform or direct-order channel.
- Do not use “Order now” if the product or branch is unavailable.

## Slogan status

`Passion for flavour` is treated as the existing working line until the rebrand decision is formally approved. Do not introduce a replacement slogan into permanent logo files without written approval.
