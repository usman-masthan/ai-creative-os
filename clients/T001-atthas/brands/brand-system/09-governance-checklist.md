# Brand Governance and Release Checklist

## Approval states

`draft → internal review → client review → approved → published → archived`

Only assets marked `approved` may enter the reusable production library. Published assets must also pass the content source-of-truth check.

## Before design

- [ ] Correct brand: master, Restaurant or Burger
- [ ] Correct branch and delivery platform scope
- [ ] Current product name, description, price and availability
- [ ] Current offer conditions and effective dates
- [ ] Approved real product photographs or clearly declared concept-image route
- [ ] Correct language and translation owner

## Brand QA

- [ ] Approved logo master and variant
- [ ] Clear space and minimum size respected
- [ ] Correct palette and sufficient contrast
- [ ] Correct typography and hierarchy
- [ ] Appropriate sub-brand personality
- [ ] One clear primary CTA
- [ ] Consistent icon style, radius and spacing
- [ ] Food presentation complies with photography rules

## Content and risk QA

- [ ] Claims supported by a named source
- [ ] Price and offer scope visible
- [ ] Ingredients and dietary/halal information verified
- [ ] Delivery platform marks current and permitted
- [ ] No third-party copyright or trademark conflict
- [ ] AI-assisted image is recorded and approved
- [ ] Final copy proofread in every language

## Export package

- Editable source file or template link
- Web and social exports in required sizes
- Print-ready PDF when applicable
- Asset manifest with owner, version and approval status
- Source/reference links and effective date
