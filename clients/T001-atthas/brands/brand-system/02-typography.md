# Typography

## Working font system

Final fonts must be confirmed against the source logo and licences. Until then, use openly licensed families to preserve portability.

| Role | Primary recommendation | Fallback | Character |
|---|---|---|---|
| Display/promotion | Oswald Bold or ExtraBold | Arial Narrow Bold, sans-serif | Condensed, confident, high-impact |
| Brand/editorial heading | Libre Baskerville Bold | Georgia Bold, serif | Heritage, trust, Restaurant warmth |
| Body/UI | Inter Regular/Medium | Arial, sans-serif | Clean, readable, multilingual-friendly |
| Prices/data | Oswald SemiBold | Arial Narrow Bold, sans-serif | Compact, scannable |

The ATTHA’S logo is artwork, not editable text. Do not recreate it by typing a substitute font.

## Hierarchy

| Level | Desktop guide | Mobile guide | Treatment |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Display | 64–96 px | 42–56 px | Oswald ExtraBold, tight leading |
| H1 | 48–64 px | 36–44 px | Bold; title case or short uppercase |
| H2 | 32–44 px | 28–34 px | Bold |
| H3 | 24–30 px | 22–26 px | SemiBold/Bold |
| Body large | 18–20 px | 17–19 px | 1.45–1.6 line height |
| Body | 16–18 px | 16–17 px | 1.5–1.65 line height |
| Caption/legal | 12–14 px | 12–14 px | Never condensed for long copy |

## Rules

- Use condensed uppercase for short promotional messages, menu groups and prices—not paragraphs.
- Limit a composition to two font families and no more than four weights.
- Keep punctuation and the apostrophe in `ATTHA’S` consistent.
- Use `ATTHA’S Burger` and `ATTHA’S Restaurant`; do not use `Atthas` or `Attha's` in branded headlines.
- Sentence case is preferred for Restaurant body copy. Burger may use short uppercase bursts.
- Maintain generous line spacing for Sinhala, Tamil and Arabic; never mechanically copy Latin line-height settings.
