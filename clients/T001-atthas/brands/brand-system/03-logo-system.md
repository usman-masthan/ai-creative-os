# Logo System

## Required approved masters

Each identity requires source-controlled SVG and PDF masters plus transparent PNG exports.

| Identity | Primary | Horizontal | Stacked | Symbol/avatar | One-colour | Reversed |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ATTHA’S master | Required | Required | Optional | A/fork mark | Required | Required |
| ATTHA’S Restaurant | Required | Required | Required | Master symbol | Required | Required |
| ATTHA’S Burger | Required | Required | Required | Master symbol or approved Burger badge | Required | Required |

## Working relationship

- The stylised `A + fork` mark is the shared endorsement symbol.
- `ATTHA’S` is always the dominant name.
- `BURGER` or `RESTAURANT` is a descriptor, not a separate unrelated logo.
- Sub-brand descriptors use an approved sans-serif treatment and may use flame gold on deep red.

## Clear space

Define `x` as the cap height of the `A` in the ATTHA’S wordmark.

- Minimum clear space: `0.5x` on every side.
- Preferred clear space: `1x` for hero placements and signage.
- No text, image edge, border, delivery badge or CTA may enter this area.

## Minimum size

- Full digital logo: minimum 160 px wide.
- Full print logo: minimum 35 mm wide.
- Symbol-only digital mark: minimum 32 px.
- Below these sizes, use the simplified approved symbol rather than shrinking the full lock-up.

## Approved colour versions

1. White wordmark + flame-gold symbol/descriptor on deep red.
2. Deep-red wordmark + flame-gold detail on white or warm cream.
3. Solid black for restricted one-colour production.
4. Solid white for dark photography, only with sufficient contrast.

## Never

- Stretch, compress, rotate, outline or add bevels.
- Add drop shadows, glows or chrome effects.
- Recolour individual letters.
- Type the wordmark using a similar font.
- Place the full-colour logo over busy food detail without a controlled brand field.
- Separate the master name and descriptor into conflicting alignments.
- Use the symbol as a decorative pattern until an approved pattern tile exists.

## File naming

`atthas-{master|restaurant|burger}-{primary|horizontal|stacked|symbol}-{colour|white|black}.{svg|pdf|png}`

Example: `atthas-burger-horizontal-colour.svg`
