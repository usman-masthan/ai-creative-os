# ATTHA'S Restaurant — Working Brand Rules

**Status:** Rebrand proposal

- Lead with appetite, variety, warmth and togetherness.
- Use Deep Red as the master anchor with Warm Cream and Herb Green for a more hospitable multi-cuisine expression.
- Product photography should feel real, generous and freshly served.
- Avoid making Restaurant look identical to Burger; Restaurant should feel broader, calmer and more welcoming.
- Never publish price, offer, opening-hour, branch or delivery claims unless resolved through the truth gate.
