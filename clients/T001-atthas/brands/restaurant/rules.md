# ATTHA’S Restaurant — Working Brand Rules

**Status:** Working brand system v0.1

## Personality

ATTHA’S Restaurant is **warm, genuine, considered and welcoming**. Use richer but restrained descriptions suited to families, groups and shared meals.

## Visual direction

- Primary palette: Warm Cream `#FFF8E8`, Deep/Ember Red `#B50008` / `#820008`, Charcoal `#171717` and restrained Toasted Gold `#F2B705`.
- Use more breathing room and softer contrast than Burger.
- Prefer table-level, overhead or intimate three-quarter compositions.
- Show serving context, tableware and shared dining when relevant.
- Represent cuisines accurately; do not mix culturally unrelated props as decoration.
- Do not ask an image model to render promotional text or the ATTHA’S logo.

## Typography

- Working editorial heading family: Libre Baskerville Bold.
- Working body/UI family: Inter.
- Promotional/data moments may use Oswald where appropriate.
- Final fonts remain subject to licence and master-brand approval.

## Copy and CTA

- Prefer descriptive, inviting language over vague luxury language.
- Avoid cultural-authenticity claims without a defined cuisine/source.
- Delivery CTAs must name the verified platform/channel.
- Typical CTA direction: `View the menu`, `Plan your visit`, or another truth-safe action suited to the campaign.

## Truth and imagery

- Never publish price, offer, opening-hour, branch or delivery claims unless resolved through the truth gate.
- Ingredients, allergens, dietary/halal claims and product availability require named-source verification.
- A real product photo or approved product-reference route is required for final product representation.
- Generic AI imagery must remain internally classified as concept imagery and may not be represented as the actual served product.
