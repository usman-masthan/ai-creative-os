# ATTHA'S Burger — Working Brand Rules

**Status:** Rebrand proposal

- Make the food the hero.
- Use tighter crops, bolder typography and more motion than the Restaurant sub-brand.
- Deep Red + Flame Gold remain the connection to ATTHA'S; Chilli Red may increase energy for Burger-only campaigns.
- Barlow Condensed Bold is proposed for high-impact campaign headings; Manrope remains the body/UI typeface.
- Prices are branch + channel facts, not universal product facts.
- Generated product imagery must not be described as the exact served product unless it is based on approved product photography/reference.
