# ATTHA’S Burger — Working Brand Rules

**Status:** Working brand system v0.1

## Personality

ATTHA’S Burger is **bold, playful, energetic and craveable**. Use short sentences, active verbs and rhythmic naming. Keep the food as the hero.

## Visual direction

- Primary palette: Deep Red `#B50008`, Flame Gold `#FFD21A`, white and charcoal.
- Use tighter, bolder compositions than Restaurant.
- Prefer eye-level or low three-quarter food hero angles.
- Maintain a strong stack silhouette and visible product layers.
- Leave deliberate negative space for deterministic copy.
- Use Flame Gold as an accent/CTA colour, not a competing full background.
- Do not ask an image model to render promotional text or the ATTHA’S logo.

## Typography

- Working display family: Oswald Bold/ExtraBold.
- Working body/UI family: Inter.
- Working price family: Oswald SemiBold.
- Final fonts remain subject to licence and master-brand approval.

## Copy and CTA

- Prefer clear, punchy invitations over exaggerated claims.
- Do not use unsupported superlatives such as “Sri Lanka’s best.”
- Delivery CTAs must name the verified platform/channel.
- Do not use `Order now` when availability is not verified.

## Truth and imagery

- Prices are branch + channel facts, not universal product facts.
- Ingredients, availability, offers and dietary/halal claims require named-source verification.
- A real product photo or approved product-reference route is required for final product representation.
- Generic AI imagery must remain internally classified as concept imagery and may not be represented as the actual served product.
